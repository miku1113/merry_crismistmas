# Privacy Policy for Merry Christmas (Santa Claus Gift Drop)

**Last Updated: December 24, 2024**

Thank you for playing "Merry Christmas" (Santa Claus Gift Drop). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

## 1. Information We Collect
We do **not** collect any personal information, location data, or usage statistics from our players. The game is designed to be played locally on your device.

## 2. Dynamic Data & Scores
The game tracks your "High Score" and personal bests. This data is stored **locally** on your device using standard storage methods (such as Unity's PlayerPrefs). We do not have access to this data, and it is not transmitted to any external servers.

## 3. Third-Party Services (Unity Ads)
This game uses **Unity Ads**, a third-party advertising service, to display optional rewarded advertisements that allow you to continue playing after game over.

**What Unity Ads Collects:**
- **Advertising ID**: Used to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance
- **Device Information**: Operating system, device model, and app version for ad compatibility
- **Ad Interaction Data**: Whether you watched, skipped, or clicked on ads

**How Ads Are Shown:**
- Ads are **ONLY** shown when you voluntarily choose to watch them by clicking a "Continue" button
- We do **NOT** show automatic, forced, or intrusive ads
- All ads can be closed within 5 seconds (rewarded ads may require watching to completion for the reward)
- We do not show interest-based advertising to children

**Unity Ads Privacy:**
Unity Ads complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR. For more information, see [Unity's Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## 4. Children's Privacy
Since we do not collect any personal information, the game is safe for players of all ages, including children.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
